Founded by Carl Pei in 2020, Nothing is a London-based consumer tech company on a mission to remove barriers between people and technology. Their radical transparency through Glyph Interface, open software philosophy, and obsessive industrial design have made them the most exciting tech brand in years.

What Makes Them Special
Glyph Interface
A revolutionary LED notification system on the rear panel — programmable, expressive, and unlike anything else in the industry.
Transparent Design
Visible internals aren't just aesthetic — they're a philosophy. Nothing believes in showing what's inside, literally and figuratively.
Nothing OS
Near-stock Android stripped of bloat, built for speed, with unique Nothing-native customisations that feel intentional — not gimmicky.
